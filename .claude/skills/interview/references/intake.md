# Intake Script

Wording, answer options, and purpose for the 11 intake questions. Suggested wording is a starting point — adapt it to her earlier answers. Offer options as a numbered list she can answer by number. Every question accepts "skip" and "not sure yet."

---

## Session 1 — Your Story (~4 min)

Open with: *"This first part takes about 4 minutes. There are no wrong answers, and you can skip anything."*

### Q1 — Last paid job
**Ask:** "What was your last paid job? Just the basics — the title, what kind of work, and roughly how long you did it."
**Options for length:** under 1 year / 1–2 / 3–5 / 6–10 / 10+ years.
**If she has none:** "No problem — lots of people start here. We'll build from what you've done outside paid work." Switch to the little-or-no-work-history tuning in `SKILL.md`.
**Feeds:** resume work history; pathway matching.

### Q2 — One thing she did well
**Ask:** "What's one thing you did well in that job? Did it involve money, people, or a number? Even a guess helps."
**Nudges to offer:** saved money / trained people / hit a goal / fixed a problem / customers asked for me.
**Follow-up (once):** "About how many / how much?"
**Feeds:** one accomplishment bullet under her last job.

### Q3 — Education and credentials
**Ask:** "Any education, certifications, or licenses? Include expired ones — lapsed counts."
**Options:** high school / some college / associate / bachelor's / master's or higher / a license or certificate (which?).
**For each lapsed credential:** note the year it lapsed. Renewal can be a fast pathway (e.g., a teaching license).
**Feeds:** resume education section; renewal pathways.

### Q4 — Time away
**Ask:** "How long have you been away from paid work?"
**Options:** under 1 year / 1–2 / 3–5 / 6–10 / 10+ years.
**Feeds:** segment tuning; gap story. 2+ years makes her eligible for Return Utah programs.

**End of Session 1:** show her profile so far (last role + strongest bullet + credentials). Ask if she wants to keep going now or come back later.

## Session 2 — Your Life Now (~4 min)

Open with: *"This part is about the time you've been home. Most people are surprised by how much they find."*

### Q5 — Activity checklist
**Ask:** "Which of these have you done while you've been home? Pick everything that applies — numbers are fine."
**Options:** the checklist in `activity-skills.md`. Show the first 8; offer "see more" for the rest.
**Follow-up:** for each pick, one short question for a number or detail ("How many volunteers, roughly?"). Skip follow-ups she declines.
**After each answer:** name the skills it shows. Keep a running count.
**Feeds:** skill profile; the career-break resume entry; volunteer resume entries; pathway matching.

### Q6 — What work needs to look like
**Ask each as its own message:**
- Hours: under 15 / 15–25 / 25–35 / full-time / not sure.
- Schedule: school hours only / flexible / evenings or weekends / any.
- Where: remote / hybrid / in-person / any.
- Childcare: "Is childcare sorted, or part of the puzzle?" — sorted / part of the puzzle / skip.
- Earliest start: now / within 3 months / 3–6 months / later.
**Feeds:** pathway filtering; resource suggestions (childcare assistance).

### Q7 — Pay that makes sense
**Ask:** "What does a job need to pay to make sense for your family?"
**Options:** hourly or yearly ranges / not sure.
**If "not sure":** note it; at the pathway step, show pay ranges and point out that the long-term view (raises, retirement, Social Security credits) matters as well as this year's childcare cost.
Use this answer to filter pathways; do not repeat the number back in written materials.

**End of Session 2:** "You just translated your time at home into N professional skills." List each skill with its evidence.

---

## Session 3 — Your Direction (~3 min)

Open with: *"Last part — about what you'd actually enjoy. About 3 minutes."*

### Q8 — Day-to-day appeal
**Ask:** "Which of these sounds most appealing day-to-day? Pick one or two."
**Options:** working with people / information and numbers / hands-on things / ideas and creativity.
**Feeds:** pathway matching (a simplified Holland-code read).

### Q9 — Environment
**Ask as two quick picks:** busy and social, or quiet and focused? Variety, or routine?

### Q10 — What matters most now
**Ask:** "What matters most right now?"
**Options:** getting back to work fastest / most room to grow / most flexibility.
**Feeds:** ranks the pathways.

### Q11 — The wildcard (optional)
**Ask:** "Anything you've always wondered if you'd be good at?"
**Feeds:** a wildcard pathway if it fits; otherwise a note for later.

**Exit:** skill profile + 2–3 pathways with honest fit reasoning (why it matches, pay range, training time and cost, flexibility). Ask which one to explore first.
