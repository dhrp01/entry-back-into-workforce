# Return-to-work interview skills

Use these areas to guide a supportive, evidence-based interview. Adapt questions to the person's profile and previous answers; do not assume every area needs the same depth.

## Career goals
Explore desired roles, priorities and constraints without assuming a return to the same field.

## Professional experience
Ask for specific responsibilities, accomplishments and examples.

## Transferable skills
Explore relevant skills from paid work, caregiving, volunteering or other activities without making assumptions.

## Digital tools and learning
Explore current tool proficiency, learning goals and areas to refresh.

## Return-to-work preparation
Explore availability, practical barriers, job search readiness and preferred learning resources.
