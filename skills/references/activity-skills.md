# Activity → Skill Mapping

Turns what she did at home into professional skills and resume bullets. Use for intake Q5 and for every resume.

## How to use

1. Show the checklist (first 8, then "see more"). She picks by number.
2. For each pick, ask the follow-up question for a number or detail.
3. Record the skills. Deduplicate across activities and keep the evidence: *"Project management — from your kitchen renovation and the soccer league."*
4. Build bullets from the templates. Every `[bracket]` is a blank she fills in. If she can't, drop that part of the bullet. Never fill a blank yourself, and never add a result she did not state.
5. After each bullet, offer **"Sounds like me"** or **"Tone it down."**

## Checklist

| # | Activity (checklist wording) | Skills | Follow-up question | Bullet template |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Managed the family budget | Budget management, financial planning, resource allocation | "About how much did the budget cover per year? Did it help you reach a goal, like paying something off?" | Managed household budget of $[amount]/year; [result she named] |
| 2 | PTA/PTO officer, room parent, or school fundraising | Stakeholder communication, event planning, fundraising, volunteer coordination | "Which role? About how much was raised, or how many volunteers?" | [Role], [school]: led fundraising that raised $[amount]; coordinated [number] volunteers |
| 3 | Organized schedules or carpools for kids' activities | Logistics coordination, scheduling, multi-priority management | "How many people or activities were you juggling in a week?" | Coordinated weekly logistics for [number] people across [number] recurring commitments |
| 4 | Coordinated care for an aging parent or family member | Case management, vendor coordination, records management, advocacy | "About how many doctors, agencies, or insurers did you deal with?" | Coordinated care across [number] medical providers, including insurance [appeals / claims] and records |
| 5 | Taught a class, led a youth group, or had a church teaching role | Public speaking, curriculum planning, group facilitation | "How many people, how often, for how long?" | Planned and taught weekly lessons for groups of [number] over [years] |
| 6 | Ran a side business (Etsy, babysitting, direct sales, crafts) | Small-business operations, customer service, sales, marketing | "What did you sell or offer? Roughly how many customers or how much in sales?" | Ran [business type]: handled pricing, inventory, and [number] customers |
| 7 | Planned a big event (reunion, wedding, large fundraiser) | Project management, budgeting, vendor negotiation | "How many guests, what budget, how many vendors?" | Planned and ran a [number]-person [event] on a $[amount] budget, working with [number] vendors |
| 8 | Managed a home renovation or a major move | Project management, contractor negotiation, timeline management | "What was the scope and budget? How many contractors?" | Managed a $[amount] [renovation / move]: chose and negotiated with [number] contractors, kept the timeline |
| 9 | Treasurer or secretary for any group | Bookkeeping, record keeping, governance, meeting management | "For what group, and how many members?" | [Treasurer / Secretary], [group] ([number] members): kept financial records and meeting minutes |
| 10 | Navigated IEPs, special-needs services, or insurance appeals | Advocacy, negotiation, complex-systems navigation, documentation | "What did you get done — services approved, an appeal won?" | Advocated through [process], resulting in [outcome she named] |
| 11 | Ran social media for a school, team, or church group | Content creation, social media management, communications | "Which accounts, how often did you post, roughly how many followers?" | Created and posted content for [group] on [platforms], [frequency] |
| 12 | Mentored or tutored kids or adults | Coaching, training, adapting to individual needs | "Who, how many, for how long?" | Tutored [number] [students / adults] in [subject] over [period] |
| 13 | Organized meal trains, service projects, or neighborhood efforts | Community organizing, coordination, leadership | "How many people took part?" | Organized [effort] with [number]+ participants |
| 14 | Coached or ran a youth sports team or league | Team leadership, scheduling, conflict resolution | "How many kids, teams, or families?" | [Coached / Ran] [league or team]: [number] teams, [number] families, season schedule |

Extend the table as new activities come up. Keep the same columns: activity, skills, follow-up, template with blanks.

## The career-break resume entry

Combine her home activities into one titled role, placed in date order above her last paid job:

```
Family Household Manager                                     2017 – 2026
- [best bullet from the checklist]
- [second-best bullet]
- [third, if strong]
```

- Title options to offer: "Family Household Manager," "Full-time Caregiver," "Career Break — Family Care." Let her pick. Avoid joke titles ("CEO of the Household").
- Formal volunteer roles with a title and an organization (PTA Vice President, league coordinator, Primary teacher) get **their own entries**, not bullets under the career break.
- Pick the bullets with the most concrete numbers first.
