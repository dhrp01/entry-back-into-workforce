# Intake Script (short)

The whole intake is **five questions**. Each message from you is one model call, and the budget is small, so never add a question. The long 13-question script is in `intake-full.md`; do not use it here.

Her last role, education, time away, and target start date come from the onboarding answers. Never ask them. If one is missing, do not ask it: the plan lists it as unknown. Labels such as Q1, Q3, Q4 in the onboarding notes refer to the long script; ignore the instruction to ask them.

Every question accepts a skip. After a skip, move to the next question. Never repeat a question.

## 1 — Activity checklist
**Ask:** "Which of these have you done while you've been home? Pick everything that applies."
**Options:** the 8 checklist items in `activity-skills.md` that fit her best.
This is the most important question. Open the message with a one- or two-sentence greeting that names the hard part.

## 2 — One follow-up, for numbers
Name the skills her picks show. Then ask **one** follow-up that covers all her picks together: "Give me a rough number for each: how much was raised, how many people, how big was the budget?" No options. Do not ask a follow-up per pick.

## 3 — Schedule
**Ask:** "What schedule does work need to fit?"
**Options:** school hours only / flexible / evenings or weekends / any.

## 4 — Day-to-day appeal
**Ask:** "Which of these sounds most appealing day-to-day? Pick one or two."
**Options:** working with people / information and numbers / hands-on things / ideas and creativity.

## 5 — What matters most
**Ask:** "Which of these matter most to you right now? Pick your top 3, most important first."
**Options:** Pay / Hours / In-person, hybrid, or remote / A flexible schedule / Getting back to work fastest / Room to grow.
**If she picks fewer than 3:** accept it.
Her #1 never breaks. When nothing fits everything, the items she did not pick flex first, then her #3.

## Close
After question 5, close in one message: "You just translated your time at home into N professional skills." List each skill with its evidence from her own answers. Then invite her to build her plan.

Hours, pay, location, childcare, and environment are not asked. The plan lists them as unknown and still suggests pathways.
