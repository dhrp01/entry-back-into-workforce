# Utah Resources

Local programs to connect her to, by need. Source: the team's vetted `UTAH_RESOURCES.md`. Use only what is listed here; for anything else, say you don't have verified details.

## How to use

- Match her need to **one to three** resources. Never list everything.
- Every suggestion ends in a single next step: "Next step: visit returnutah.utah.gov and check the next cohort date."
- Program details change. Say "check the site for current dates and eligibility" instead of promising openings, amounts, or acceptance.

## Return-to-work programs

| Program | What it offers | Link |
|---|---|---|
| **Return Utah** | State-run, 16-week **paid** returnships in state government for Utah residents with a career break of 2+ years, for any reason. Includes resume, interview, job-search, and LinkedIn workshops. Most participants are women returning after raising children. | returnutah.utah.gov |
| **Tech-Moms** | 9-week program for women to gain tech-industry skills; 500+ graduates. Free "Better Way Thursdays" virtual workshops. | tech-moms.org |
| **Women Tech Council — AI Ready Returnship (AIR)** | Structured relaunch program for experienced professionals re-entering an AI-enabled workplace. | womentechcouncil.com |
| **UAMMI Returnship** | Return-to-work hiring and mentoring for women in advanced materials and manufacturing. | uammi.org |

## Coaching and training money

| Resource | What it offers | Link |
|---|---|---|
| **Utah Dept. of Workforce Services (DWS)** | Free one-on-one career coaching; funding for short-term training, certificates, apprenticeships, and degrees for eligible applicants; resume and interview help. Walk-in employment centers statewide. | jobs.utah.gov |
| **Talent Ready Utah / Utah Works** | Industry training programs tied to employer needs. | business.utah.gov |
| **Apprenticeship Utah** | Paid on-the-job learning plus classroom instruction. | apprenticeship.utah.gov |
| **Local library systems** | Free LinkedIn Learning access, resume help, career coaching. | her county library |

## Childcare

| Resource | What it offers | Link |
|---|---|---|
| **Utah Child Care Assistance** (DWS Office of Child Care) | Subsidy for working or studying parents up to 85% of state median income; sliding co-pay. Apply through myCase. | jobs.utah.gov/occ · mycase.utah.gov |
| **Kids in Care** | Free drop-in childcare for recently unemployed parents while they job hunt; DWS pays for up to 150 hours. | jobs.utah.gov |
| **Care About Childcare** | Statewide provider search with profiles and quality ratings. | careaboutchildcare.utah.gov |

## Other help

| Resource | What it offers | Link |
